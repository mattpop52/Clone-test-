Boosting Web Demos with CodePen’s SlideVars

21.01.2026

Interactive web demos are a great way to engage users, giving them hands-on experience with your product or service. CodePen’s SlideVars is a nifty new tool that can take this experience to the next level. Let’s explore how it can benefit your business.

What’s CodePen’s SlideVars and why is it important?

CodePen’s SlideVars is a feature that enables you to create interactive UI panels right in your web demo. This allows you to adjust CSS variables live, making your demos more dynamic and engageing. Such interactivity not only impresses users but also helps them gain a deeper understanding of what you are showcasing.

How can I implement SlideVars in my project?

To use SlideVars, import it into the JavaScript panel with: import { slideVars } from "@codepen/slidevars";. Then, simply initialise it with slideVars.init();. Make sure your CSS variables are declared on the :root element to ensure automatic panel detection.

What are the benefits of using SlideVars for web demos?

Using SlideVars brings several advantages, such as enhanced interactivity, ease of use, and instant feedback on changes. Users can tweak settings on the go, allowing them to engage directly with various elements without needing deep technical knowledge.

When should I consider using SlideVars in my web projects?

Consider using SlideVars when you want to demonstrate products or services interactively or visually explain complex ideas. It’s especially useful during presentations or on websites where engageing customers is a priority.

How can SlideVars improve user engagement?

SlideVars enhances user engagement by making demos more hands-on and responsive. When users can control parts of a demo themselves, they feel more connected, rather than just passively watching pre-set animations or configurations.

What mistakes should I avoid when using SlideVars?

Avoid cluttering your demo with too many variable adjustments, as this might overwhelm users. Start simple and gradually add complexity based on user feedback.

What’s the difference between manual configuration and default mode in SlideVars?

The key difference is in customization: manual configuration allows you to set input type, range, default value, unit type, and more, while default mode automatically detects CSS variables with minimal setup but offers less flexibility.

Do I need advanced coding skills to use SlideVars effectively?

No advanced coding skills are needed as long as you follow the basic setup instructions provided by CodePen. That said, having some familiarity with HTML and CSS will help you make the most of it.

Can I customise where the SlideVar panel appears in my demo?

You can control the panel’s position by placing a custom element like <slide-vars><p>Custom Label!</p></slide-vars>. By default, it appears at the bottom of the HTML body, but customisation offers flexibility according to your design needs.

What are scroll-driven animations and why are they important?

Scroll-driven animations react to user scrolling, creating visual effects that capture attention and guide users. For e-commerce, they enhance navigation and keep users interested as they browse products.

These animations boost aesthetics, aid storytelling, and create smoother transitions. They can lead to longer visits and potentially more sales.

How do I use GSAP for scroll-driven animations?

To create scroll-driven animations, use GSAP with its ScrollTrigger plugin. Start by adding GSAP scripts to your site, then identify elements to animate using data attributes.

Set up the HTML for ScrollSmoother, style animations with CSS, and use GSAP’s JavaScript functions to manage scrolling effects smoothly.

What benefits do scroll-driven animations offer?

These animations boost engagement by making browsing interactive. They highlight key products and offers as users navigate.

The animations can increase dwell time by quickly capturing interest, aligning with product discovery and storytelling. They make your site memorable, setting it apart from competitors.

What’s the cost of implementing these animations?

A DIY approach using GSAP and forums can cut costs, but professionals often deliver tailored, high-quality results.

Review annually to stay competitive. Adapt designs as trends and market directions shift. Regular updates ensure your site remains fresh and engageing.

Balancing SEO and PPC: Your Questions Answered

05.01.2026

Navigating digital marketing is often tricky for UK SMEs trying to balance SEO and PPC. Achieving sustainable growth needs a strategic approach to budget allocation. Here’s how to make the most of these channels.

SEO vs PPC

SEO enhances your organic search rankings, while PPC pays for ads to appear at the top of search results.

SEO is a long-term investment in content and technical improvements, delivering organic traffic over time. PPC offers immediate visibility and is directly linked to performance metrics like clicks and conversions, albeit at a cost.

Why Balance SEO and PPC?

Balancing SEO and PPC is crucial for UK SMEs as it leverages both immediate and long-term marketing benefits.

SEO builds brand authority and reduces cost per lead over time. PPC provides quick wins and predictable performance. Together, they form a comprehensive strategy adaptable to business goals.

Budget Allocation Tips

Assess your immediate needs and long-term goals, then allocate funds to PPC for quick results and SEO for lasting impact.

A 70/30 or 60/40 split favouring PPC is common initially, especially if immediate traffic is needed. As organic efforts gain traction, the balance can shift towardss SEO.

SEO Benefits

SEO offers sustainable growth by improving organic search visibility and reducing long-term acquisition costs.

Over time, as rankings improve, SEO delivers a steady stream of traffic without extra ad spend, making it cost-effective in the long run.

PPC Advantages

PPC provides immediate visibility and measurable results, ideal for quick traffic boosts and short-term goals.

It’s especially useful when launching new products or campaigns, allowing for precise targeting and budget control.

When to Prioritise SEO

Prioritise SEO when aiming for long-term brand visibility and reduced customer acquisition costs.

If your goals involve building brand authority or improving organic reach, investing more in SEO will pay off beyond initial campaigns.

Common Budget Mistakes

Avoid underestimating the time SEO takes to show results and over-relying on PPC for long-term growth.

Set realistic expectations; SEO isn’t a quick fix, and PPC can become costly if not managed carefully. Regularly review and adjust your budget based on performance.

Sustainable Growth with SEO and PPC

SEO and PPC together drive sustainable growth by complementing each other — SEO builds long-term value, while PPC offers immediate returns.

This synergy captures quick wins and invests in your future, keeping your business competitive in the digital landscape.

Digital Marketing Trends to Keep an Eye on in 2026

01.01.2026

Now then, as we step into 2026, the digital marketing landscape is evolving faster than a Yorkshire terrier on a hot summer’s day. It’s no longer about just dipping your toes in the water—it’s about properly diving in and making a splash. If you’re feeling a tad overwhelmed, don’t fret. We’re here to guide you through the top digital marketing trends that you need to master to keep up with the changing tides.

Conversational Search Redefines SEO

SEO has been given a good shake-up. The days of keyword-stuffing are long gone. Now, it’s all about conversational queries. With the likes of Google and Bing introducing AI-driven search functionalities, the goal is to provide users with answers directly within the search engine. This means creating content that is structured around providing clear, authoritative answers. No messing with those pesky keyword rankings—focus on the audience’s intent and deliver valuable information.

The Video-Commerce Boom

Video content is fast becoming the cornerstone of online shopping experiences. Brands are using video not just for storytelling, but to drive purchases through interactive elements and live shopping features. The numbers don’t lie—by 2026, a significant chunk of video ads will be AI-generated, and social commerce revenues are on the rise. The challenge? Create video content that not just engages but converts, integrating eCommerce features directly into videos.

The Privacy-First Data Revolution

Privacy concerns are reshaping the landscape. With third-party cookies getting the chop and tighter regulations setting in, it’s crucial for businesses to shift their focus to first-party data. This means leveraging direct interactions with customers to build a robust data strategy. Think of it as creating a ‘data spine’ that supports your entire marketing framework.

What this means for businesses

These emerging trends underline the importance of adapting to new technologies and consumer expectations. Businesses need to embrace conversational content strategies, enhance their video marketing approaches, and prioritise privacy-compliant data practices. This strategic realignment is essential to remain competitive and deliver a seamless customer experience.

What we’d do next

  • Evaluate SEO Strategies: Transition from keyword-focused to answer-oriented content.
  • Enhance Video Marketing: Integrate interactive elements and purchase triggers into video content.
  • Strengthen Data Practices: Prioritize first-party data and build a solid data management framework.

Our take

The digital marketing game is changing, but that’s not to say it’s all doom and gloom. These trends present brilliant opportunities for businesses willing to innovate and invest in the reet tools and strategies. Embrace them early and you’ll be ahead of the curve, steering your marketing efforts in a grand new direction.
