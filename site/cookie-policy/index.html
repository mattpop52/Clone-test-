Welcome to our website

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience and to help us understand how you use our site. By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to help us analyse how you use our website and to improve your experience. We may also use cookies to personalise content and ads, to provide social media features, and to analyse our traffic. We may also share information about your use of our site with our social media, advertising, and analytics partners.

Types of cookies we use There are two main types of cookies that we use on our website:

  • Necessary cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Analytics cookies: These cookies help us understand how you use our website, so that we can improve our services. We use tools like Google Analytics to collect information about your visits to our website, including the pages you visit, the links you click, and other actions you take.

How to control cookies

You can control cookies by adjusting your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, so please review it regularly. If we make significant changes to this policy, we will notify you by posting a notice on our website or by other means.

Contact us If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at support@our-agency.co.uk.
